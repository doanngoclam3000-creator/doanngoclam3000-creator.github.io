  Keo bieu tuong "Soan Bai Website" tha vao thu muc Applications ben canh.

LAN MO DAU TIEN:
  1. Vao Applications, BAM CHUOT PHAI vao "Soan Bai Website" -> chon Open
  2. Bam Open mot lan nua
  3. May hoi quyen truy cap thu muc Tai lieu -> bam CHO PHEP

  - Thu muc website nam o: ~/Documents/WebPhanMem
