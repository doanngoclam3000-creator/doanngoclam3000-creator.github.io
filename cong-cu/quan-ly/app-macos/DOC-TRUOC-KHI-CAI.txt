QUAN LY WEBSITE
===============

CACH CAI:
  Keo bieu tuong "Quan Ly Website" tha vao thu muc Applications ben canh.

LAN MO DAU TIEN:
  1. Vao Applications, BAM CHUOT PHAI vao "Quan Ly Website" -> chon Open
  2. Bam Open mot lan nua
  3. May hoi quyen truy cap thu muc va o ngoai -> bam CHO PHEP
  (Chi can lam vay lan dau. Nhung lan sau mo binh thuong.)

YEU CAU:
  - macOS 12 tro len
  - Da cai Node.js (tai o nodejs.org, chon ban LTS)
  - Cam o CODE vao may: ma nguon website nam o
      /Volumes/CODE/DuAn/WebPhanMem
    (De cho khac thi phan mem se hoi de ban tu chon)
  - Muon day file cai dat len GitHub thi can cong cu gh da dang nhap:
      ~/bin/gh auth login

PHAN MEM LAM DUOC GI:
  - Tong quan: soat loi ep cap nhat, link tai hong, tinh hinh dung web
  - Bai viet: viet, sua, xoa, keo tha anh va video
  - Phan mem: sua moi truong cua trang phan mem, gia, an/hien, gioi han ban cu
  - Phat hanh: xem tung he dieu hanh dang o ban nao, kiem link tai, ep cap nhat
  - Vi va lien ket: day bang link tai sang may chu vi tren Cloudflare
  - Thong ke: luot xem tung trang, luot tai tung phan mem
  - Cau hinh: sua thong tin chung cua website

PHIM TAT:
  Cmd + S  luu muc dang sua
  Cmd + R  tai lai
  Cmd + P  mo website xem thu
